## 3. Data, Databases & User Outputs Boundary

1. **User Scientific Outputs:** The rights holder claims no copyright ownership over
   molecular coordinates, chemical structures, reaction schemes, input decks, or quantum
   chemistry computational outputs generated by users of the platform.
2. **Curated IR Reference Database (`data/ir_peak_database.json`):** Curated compilation of
   vibrational frequency reference data derived from open scientific literature, standard
   spectroscopic tables, and computational benchmarks. Spectroscopic facts, physical
   constants, and published scientific data remain public domain / open scholarly reference.
3. **Third-Party Chemical Databases:** Compound structure queries retrieve publicly accessible
   data from the National Library of Medicine (PubChem / NCBI) under open government data
   terms.
