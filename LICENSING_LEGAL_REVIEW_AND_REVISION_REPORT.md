## Part I: Answers to the 18 Legal Review Questions

### 1. Ownership and Rights Holder Identity
- **Single Rights Holder:** The proprietary codebase of ORCA Web Lab is authored, owned, and controlled exclusively by **Abdulsalam S. Hasan**.
- **No External Co-Owners:** There are currently no external co-owners, employees, contractors, corporations, or third-party developers holding proprietary rights in the codebase.
- **Copyright Notice:** `Copyright (c) 2026 Abdulsalam S. Hasan. All rights reserved.`

### 2. Free Non-Commercial Use Tiers & Permitted Scope
The license explicitly grants free-of-charge permissions for:
- Personal learning, self-study, and non-commercial experimentation;
- Classroom instruction, coursework, student laboratory exercises, and educational training;
- Student theses, dissertations, and degree requirements;
- Independent academic research resulting in open, publicly accessible scholarly publications;
- Scientific benchmarking and algorithm comparisons not conducted for commercial advantage;
- Local non-commercial modifications for academic laboratory workflows.

### 3. Nature-and-Purpose Non-Commercial Definition
- **Standard of Use:** Non-commercial eligibility is defined strictly by the **nature and purpose of the activity**, not solely by the organization's legal form.
- **Explicit University Notice:** Status as an accredited university, non-profit institution, hospital, or student does **not** automatically render an activity non-commercial. If an academic laboratory performs sponsored corporate research or fee-for-service consulting, that activity is legally classified as Commercial Use.

### 4. Definition of Commercial Use & Internal Corporate R&D
- **Comprehensive Commercial Scope:** Any activity that is not strictly non-commercial is defined as Commercial Use.
- **Internal Corporate R&D:** The license explicitly states that internal corporate research (e.g. molecular screening, drug discovery, materials modeling within a pharmaceutical or chemical corporation) constitutes Commercial Use requiring a Commercial License, even if the software itself is not sold or redistributed to third parties.

### 5. Sponsored Research & Contract Research
- **Classification:** Research workflows funded by, contracted by, or delivering proprietary work products to for-profit commercial entities are classified as Commercial Use.
- **Permitted Academic Research:** Only genuinely non-commercial research resulting in open, unrestricted public scientific publications qualifies under the free academic grant.

### 6. Modification, Redistribution & Sublicensing Rules
- **Permitted Non-Commercial Redistribution:** Users may redistribute unmodified or modified copies for permitted non-commercial purposes, provided:
  1. Original copyright and full `LICENSE` notices are retained intact;
  2. Substantial modifications are prominently documented with date and description;
  3. No endorsement by Abdulsalam S. Hasan or ORCA Web Lab is implied;
  4. Terms of redistribution are no broader than License v1.1;
  5. `THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES.md` is bundled.
- **Sublicensing Prohibition:** Users cannot sublicense commercial rights, sell copies, grant SaaS rights, or grant enterprise rights. Downstream recipients receive rights directly from the author under this License.

### 7. Commercial Licensing Framework
- Commercial Use is strictly prohibited under the Academic License.
- Commercial exploitation requires executing a separate, written **Commercial License Agreement** covering Enterprise deployments, SaaS operations, commercial R&D, OEM integration, or consulting. Commercial terms and fees are negotiated separately.

### 8. Future Intellectual Property Assignment Framework
- **Not a Licensing Tier:** IP Assignment is a separate legal transaction transferring proprietary ownership.
- **Explicit Separation:** The Academic License does not transfer ownership; Commercial Licenses do not automatically transfer ownership.
- **Written Contract Required:** Any transfer of ownership requires an independent written Intellectual Property Assignment Agreement specifically identifying the rights being assigned.

### 9. Treatment of Patents and Proprietary Rights Wording
- **Qualified Language:** The license avoids unsupported patent claims and uses the legally precise formulation: *"copyright and other proprietary intellectual-property rights owned or controlled by the rights holder, to the extent legally transferable."*

### 10. Status of Pre-Existing Valid Licenses
- **Non-Retroactive Cancellation:** A future sale or assignment of proprietary intellectual property does not automatically or retroactively cancel valid pre-existing academic licenses. Any successor acquires title subject to valid existing grants in accordance with applicable law.

### 11. Third-Party Software Inventory and Boundaries
- `THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES.md` provides a comprehensive inventory of all runtime, frontend, font, and cloud dependencies (Flask, RDKit, Cryptography, 3Dmol.js, OpenPyXL, Pillow, etc.).
- Third-party components remain governed exclusively by their respective licenses (MIT, BSD-3-Clause, Apache-2.0, OFL-1.1) and are not modified, assigned, or sublicensed by the author.

### 12. ORCA Quantum Chemistry Software Boundary
- ORCA Web Lab is an independent platform that interfaces with ORCA.
- ORCA is proprietary software of Prof. Frank Neese et al. (Max Planck Institute für Kohlenforschung / FACCTs GmbH).
- ORCA Web Lab does **not** bundle or distribute ORCA binaries. Users obtain their own authorized copy directly from the official ORCA forum under the ORCA EULA.
- ORCA Web Lab grants zero rights over ORCA itself.

### 13. Scientific Data, Constants, and User Outputs
- The author claims zero copyright ownership over user-uploaded molecular coordinates, user-designed reactions, or computed quantum chemistry results.
- Public domain physical constants, literature references, and curated spectroscopic tables (`data/ir_peak_database.json`) remain open scholarly reference.
